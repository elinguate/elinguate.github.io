<center><h1>Audio Game Hub</h1></center>
<br>
Audio Game Hub is a small games hub designed for non-sighted players - an app where your vision isn't required to navigate, understand, win, or even beat your friends!

As a student I joined a small team making this ambitiously accessible game - and I was able to drive not only a large portion of the technical development within Unity, but also the games' designs and our overall aesthetic. With a core iconography of sonic ripples, AGH focuses on minimalist graphics that can accompany the audio experience rather than becoming the center themselves, and was built for a variety of platforms (iOS, Android, etc.). With 50K+ downloads it was so wonderful to see our work not only out in the wild, but also have a genuine impact on the community we were trying to reach!
