<center><h1>PureDepth</h1></center>
<br>
PureDepth’s MLD technology is an innovative 3D display that utilises two distinct LCD layers and clever content to create the illusion of seamless, real materials. Their target focus was creating a luxury screen and proof of concepts for premier Automotive OEMs. As a Software Engineer on the team, we assisted and sometimes led the design and construction of interactive demos that were widely viewed privately and publicly at exhibitions such as CES 2017, 2018, and 2019. Our work also helped land MLD as Elektronik’s Automotive Product of the Year 2017.

Working within Unity, we created, iterated, and refined the core technology and tools that led the production process for MLD content. Over the many rapid-fire projects we were involved with, we leveraged our expertise and flair for excellence in a variety of fields, such as shader construction, UI/UX design, and hardware integration.

After PureDepth was closed - I also coordinated and led a remote multi-disciplinary team in New Zealand to deliver a demonstration for Aptiv's CES 2020 showcase. Utilising Unity and a bevy of real-time and pre-rendered assets we delivered a multi-screen display to show off Aptiv's computer vision advances.
