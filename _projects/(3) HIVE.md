<center><h1>HIVE</h1></center>
<br>
As part of an exhibition day at Refinery NZ, a standalone VR game was developed to show off the importance and complexity of the daily ordering process for the refinery. By leveraging the sheer joy of just playing in VR - we built an experience that was lightweight, flexible, and focused on the fun - as the vast majority of users were not experienced with XR technology. Player had to pick and choose the right combination of crude oil supplies to ensure their final product was of high quality!

While a very short (and contained) project, it was a great opportunity to play more with VR in Unity - and to build clean and stylized gameplay from planning to implementation!
